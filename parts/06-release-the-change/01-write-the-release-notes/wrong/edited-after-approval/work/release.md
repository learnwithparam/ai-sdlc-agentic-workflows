# Release notes: broken bikes leave the map, and crews fix the most urgent first

## Riders

- When you report a broken bike, we take it off the list of bikes you can rent.
- This happens within one minute.
- The station count shows one less bike ready to ride, so you will not walk to a bike that does not work.
- Our crews now fix the most urgent bikes first, so the bikes you report get fixed faster.
- Thank you for telling us about broken bikes. Every report helps keep riders safe.
- Riders in every part of Brannock get this change today.

## Crews

- The crew queue now puts the job that is due first on top. When two jobs are due at the same time, the older job comes first.
- The 48 hour clock starts when the rider reports the bike, not when the job is opened.
- A reported bike is taken out of service at the same moment its repair job is created.
