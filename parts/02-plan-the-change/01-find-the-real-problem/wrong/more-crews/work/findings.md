# Adding Repair Crews to Meet the 48-Hour Repair Limit

## Why more crews will reduce the misses

- **More repair hours each day.** Under SLA-4, a bike wrenched by a rider must be repaired within 48 hours of the report. Two more crews add repair hours every shift, so more of the queue gets cleared before that deadline.
- **Shorter waits before work starts.** When there are more jobs open than crews to take them, jobs wait in the queue and use up their 48 hours before anyone touches them. With more crews, each job waits less and has more of its time left for the repair itself.
- **Room to absorb busy periods.** Wrench reports come in uneven bursts. Extra crews give the operation spare capacity, so a busy day doesn't spill into missed deadlines over the following days.

## Recommendation

Hire two more repair crews so that wrench reports are consistently repaired within the 48-hour limit set by SLA-4.
