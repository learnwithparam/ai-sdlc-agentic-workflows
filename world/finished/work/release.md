# Release notes: broken bikes leave the map

## Riders

When you report a broken bike, it leaves the map at once. The next rider will not take it by mistake.
Thank you for every report. It helps us fix bikes faster.

## Crews

Your queue now shows the job that is due first at the top. The 48 hour clock starts when the rider reports the bike, not when the job is opened.
Inspection jobs still have 96 hours.
