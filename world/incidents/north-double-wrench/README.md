# The double wrench incident in North district

These are the raw logs from the incident. Part 7 of the course turns them into a postmortem.

- `incident.log` is the on-call log, one line per event.
- `deploys.log` is the deploy log for the spoke service.
- `chat.txt` is the team chat from the incident channel.
- `availability.csv` is the share of North district bikes riders could rent, every 10 minutes.

For example, the line `07:40 retry storm begins` in `incident.log` is where the timeline starts.
